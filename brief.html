Digital Operations & AI Automation

A paid starting role: own the website and the path from a customer’s first message to the team’s next action, then automate the parts that pass testing.

Every AI step is measured before it ships, and a person approves anything a customer sees. The scope grows as the results justify it.

What I’ll own

  • Website content and public-information upkeep.
  • Inquiry records, draft replies, and follow-up preparation.
  • AI-assisted triage and drafting, evaluated before release and monitored after.
  • Documentation and a weekly update on delivery, decisions, and next actions.

Already built

  • A 24-section website review with 38 prioritized actions.
  • An AI triage pipeline tested against 40 hand-labeled inquiries, with published code and results.
  • Office templates, trackers, and a public facts register.
  • The proposal site, designed, built, and deployed with AI-assisted development.

The first 90 days

Days 1–30Learn and deliver. Validate public facts, revise the homepage and two service pages, map the inquiry process, and measure a baseline.
Days 31–60Own and test. Take over two routines, specify an inquiry form with a location field, and rerun the AI evaluation on approved, redacted real inquiries.
Days 61–90Pilot and decide. Run a limited AI-assisted triage pilot with every reply approved by a person, review time, corrections, and cost, then recommend whether to expand, adjust, or stop.

Release gates for any AI pilot

Every emergency goes to a person. No prices, promises, or safety advice appear in drafts. At least 95% of routes and extracted fields are correct. Every reply is approved before sending, and monthly spend is capped. We agree the final targets after the baseline.

How we’ll judge the work

Median time to the first human response, handoff completeness, follow-up coverage, and handling time including review and corrections, all measured against the day-30 baseline.

The decision to make together

Choose one initial assignment, agree paid hours and compensation, name the approval contact, and set the first review date. A 30-minute conversation is enough to decide whether this role fits.
