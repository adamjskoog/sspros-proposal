Digital Operations & AI Automation

I’d like to join your team in a paid role looking after the website, customer follow-up, and simple AI tools that save time.

I’m new to the electrical business, and I won’t pretend otherwise. I’d start by learning how you already work, then help wherever you tell me it’s useful. You’d approve anything a customer sees.

What I’d take care of

  • Running the AI tools. I set them up, keep them working, and fix them when something changes. No one in the office has to touch the technical side.
  • Checking the AI’s work. I review a sample of its drafts every week and adjust it when it gets something wrong.
  • Keeping the website current. Services, photos, and facts get updated soon after you approve a change.
  • Staying within the $400 limit. I watch both subscriptions and let you know before anything would change.
  • A short weekly update. One page in plain language: what was handled, what the AI got wrong, and what’s next.

Already built

  • A short website review with 38 ideas to talk through.
  • An AI step that reads a customer message, sorts it, and drafts a reply for a person to approve. On a 40-message practice test it handled all 40 correctly, sent 6/6 emergencies to a person, and wrote 0 unsafe replies.
  • Office templates, trackers, and a single sheet of approved company facts.
  • This proposal website, built and published with the help of AI tools.

The honest limit

The practice messages were ones I wrote myself. Before anyone relies on the AI step, I’d test it on your real customer messages, with your team checking the answers. If it doesn’t meet the bar we agree on, it doesn’t go live.

The first 90 days

Days 1–30Learn first. Check the company facts, suggest homepage wording and a draft service page, learn how customer messages are handled, and measure how things work today.
Days 31–60Own and test. Take over two regular tasks, plan a better contact form that asks where the job is, and test the AI step on real messages, with names removed and the team checking the answers.
Days 61–90Try it and decide. Try the AI tool on a small scale with every reply approved by a person, review time saved, corrections, and cost, then recommend whether to expand, adjust, or stop.

How we’ll judge the work

How long customers wait for a first reply from a person, how often messages arrive complete, whether every open request has someone responsible, and time spent per message. All are compared with the starting measurement from the first 30 days.

AI tool costs: $400 a month, never more

The company provides and pays for two AI subscriptions in its own name: Claude Max ($200) and ChatGPT Pro with Codex ($200). No pay-as-you-go credits, add-ons, or usage fees. If a limit is reached, the work waits. Any change needs your written approval.

The decision to make together

Choose one first job, agree on paid hours and pay, name who approves the work, and set the first review date. A 30-minute conversation is enough to decide whether this role fits.
