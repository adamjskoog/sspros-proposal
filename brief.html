A measured start

The first 90 days

Weeks 1–2: listen

Learn how a customer request moves through your office and which software you already use. Bring back a short list of suggestions for you to choose from.

By day 30: first results

One website fix you approved, live and checked. One simple follow-up habit in the system you already use, so no customer is forgotten.

By day 60: one AI task

Try the AI idea you picked on real work, with a person checking everything it produces. Keep track of the time it takes, including the checking.

Day 90: decide together

Keep it, change it, or stop it, based on what actually happened. Agree the ongoing role, hours, and pay.

What we’d watch: how quickly customers hear back, whether requests arrive complete, that nothing is forgotten, and the time spent, including checking the AI’s work.
