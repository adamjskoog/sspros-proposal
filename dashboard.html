              How it would work

              1. Comes inWebsite form, email, voicemail (turned into text), or a text message.
              2. Safety checkSimple rules look for hazard words in English and Spanish. If anything matches, it goes straight to a person. This runs even if the AI gets it wrong.
              3. AI helper preparesPulls out what it’s about, where, and when; notes what’s missing; drafts a polite reply. It never sets prices, times, or safety advice.
              4. Sorted into a laneElectrical, Communications, or Website, each with a named person over it.
              5. A person decidesEdits and approves the reply, reassigns, or escalates. Every step is logged.
              6. Goes out the usual wayApproved replies go from your normal email or phone, not from the AI.

              What’s real here: the website messages marked “Saved AI answer” are what the AI actually produced in testing (40 made-up messages; every emergency went to a person). Items marked “Written example” show how calls, emails, and texts would look.

              Before it touches real messages: we’d agree the rules with you, test on approved examples, and start with a person reviewing everything. We’d also check whether the software you already use can do part of this.
