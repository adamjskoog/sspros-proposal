04 / THE FIRST 90 DAYS

A focused start.
More responsibility as it works.

First we agree on hours, priorities, and what I can access. At each checkpoint we look at the finished work together and decide what comes next.

Days 1–30 · Learn & deliver

Learn first.

Learn how customer messages are handled today, agree on my first jobs, and measure how things work now so we can compare later.

WHAT I’D AIM FOR
  • A sheet of company facts for you to check, and a short list of website ideas we agree on.
  • Suggested homepage wording and one draft service page, for you to review.
  • A simple map of how a customer message moves through the office, and who handles each step.
  • A starting measurement of reply times, missing details, and time spent per message.
Review at day 30

Are the drafts accurate and useful? Which office routine should we improve first?

Days 31–60 · Own & test

Take on regular tasks.

Take over two regular tasks, then test the AI tool on real company messages before anyone relies on it.

WHAT I’D AIM FOR
  • Two tasks I handle every week: keeping the website current and following up on customer messages.
  • Office templates adjusted to how you work, if they’re useful, and contact-form ideas for you to approve.
  • The AI test run again on real messages you approve, with names removed, and the results reviewed together.
  • Agreed rules: how good the tool must be before use, how customer information is handled, and a monthly spending limit.
Review at day 60

Does the AI tool meet the bar on real messages? If not, what would it take?

Days 61–90 · Try it & decide

Show the results. Choose what’s next.

Try the AI tool on a small scale alongside the way things are done now, and compare the results with the starting measurement.

WHAT I’D AIM FOR
  • If the test results are good, a small trial of the AI tool, with every reply approved by a person.
  • A before-and-after review covering time, corrections, and cost.
  • Short how-to instructions, a backup person who knows the routine, and a way to do it by hand if needed.
  • A clear recommendation to expand, adjust, or stop, plus a list of what to tackle next.
Review at day 90

Which responsibilities become permanent? Agree the ongoing role, scope, and compensation.
