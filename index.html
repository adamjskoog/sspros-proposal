03 / The first 90 days

Deliver something useful.
Build from there.

Each phase ends with a review of finished work and a decision about the next responsibility.

Days 1–30

Learn & deliver

Complete the agreed service page, learn how customer requests move through the office today, and measure how long it takes.

Review: Is the page useful, and which recurring task should I own next?

Days 31–60

Own & test

Own website updates and follow-up preparation. Test the AI tool on real company emails, with names and contact details removed, against checks we agree on.

Review: Does it reduce work once checking and corrections are included?

Days 61–90

Compare & decide

If the test goes well, try it on a small number of new requests, with a person approving every reply. Write down the routine and compare time, quality, and cost.

Review: Expand, adjust, or stop the trial; agree the ongoing role.

Four measures: how quickly customers hear back, whether requests arrive with the details you need, whether every request has someone responsible and a next step, and staff time per email.

The responsibilities and decisions we would agree

What I would own

  • Website wording, updates, and routine checks.
  • Records of customer requests, draft replies, and follow-up reminders.
  • Testing and maintenance of approved AI tools.
  • Short instructions and a weekly progress update.

What we would agree

  • Paid hours, compensation, manager, and first review date.
  • Who approves public copy and outgoing replies.
  • Account access and data handling. Tool costs have a fixed limit, shown below.
  • How we judge finished work, and how we go back to doing it by hand if a trial isn’t working.
