Prepared for System Solutions Pros · November 2026

Digital Operations
& Website Support

A paid starting role to make public information clearer, organize customer inquiries, and keep the next action moving.

I’d like to own a focused set of digital and administrative responsibilities, learn how the business operates, and expand the scope as the work proves useful.

What I would own

  • Website content and approved public-information upkeep.
  • Assigned inquiry records, follow-up preparation, and draft responses.
  • Practical process improvements and clear operating instructions.
  • A concise weekly update on delivery, decisions, and next actions.

Work already prepared

  • A 24-section website review with 38 prioritized actions.
  • Blank trackers, office templates, and a public facts register.
  • An interactive inquiry-handoff prototype with missing-detail and human-escalation examples.
  • A phased delivery plan with measurable review points.

The first 90 days

Days 1–30Learn and deliver. Validate public facts, prepare homepage and two service drafts, map the current inquiry process, and establish baseline measures.
Days 31–60Own and improve. Maintain two agreed routines, adapt three templates, specify the inquiry form, and test a limited handoff improvement with human review.
Days 61–90Measure and expand. Review results, document the routines and backup ownership, and recommend what to continue, adjust, or stop.

How we would judge the work

Review accuracy, useful delivery, and ownership. Where records allow, compare median first-human-response time, handoff completeness, follow-up coverage, and handling time including corrections. Agree targets after the baseline; do not assume revenue or time savings.

Where automation fits

Start with the existing tools and a clear workflow. A later AI pilot could extract inquiry details and prepare drafts from approved information. Evaluate it on approved, redacted examples; keep review, technical judgment, pricing, scheduling, and customer commitments with the team.

The decision to make together

Choose one initial assignment, agree paid hours and compensation, name the approval contact, and set the first review date. A 30-minute conversation can establish whether this starting role fits the company’s needs.

Scope depends on agreed hours, access, approvals, and available information. This is an independent candidate proposal, not an official company publication.
