A measured start

The first 90 days

Days 1–30: deliver and measure

Map the inquiry path, deliver the agreed website change, and run one follow-up routine. Record the starting measurements.

Days 31–60: own and evaluate

Maintain recurring tasks. With company-approved data rules, test assistance privately and compare quality, review effort, and corrections.

Days 61–90: trial and decide

If the evidence meets the agreed bar, run a supervised trial. Review every customer reply, document fallback and cover, then recommend expanding, adjusting, or stopping.

Measure: first human reply time in business hours, required details, unowned or overdue next actions, and handling minutes including review and corrections.
