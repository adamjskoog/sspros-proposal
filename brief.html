Digital Operations & AI Automation

I’d like to join your team in a paid role looking after the website, customer follow-up, and simple AI tools that save time.

I’m new to the electrical business, and I won’t pretend otherwise. I’d start by learning how you already work, then help wherever you tell me it’s useful. You’d approve anything a customer sees.

What the AI service does

  • Sorts each new request. Service, location, and timing, picked out and sent to the right person.
  • Flags missing details. So the team asks once, early, instead of going back and forth.
  • Drafts a reply. Ready for a person to check, edit, and send. Nothing goes out on its own.
  • Keeps follow-ups from slipping. Every open request has someone responsible and a date for the next step.

What I’d take care of

  • Running the AI tools. I set them up, keep them working, and fix them when something changes. No one in the office has to touch the technical side.
  • Checking the AI’s work. I review a sample of its drafts every week and adjust it when it gets something wrong.
  • Keeping the website current. Services, photos, and facts get updated soon after you approve a change.
  • Staying within the $400 limit. I watch both subscriptions and let you know before anything would change.
  • A short weekly update. One page in plain language: what was handled, what the AI got wrong, and what’s next.

Already built

  • A short website review with 38 ideas to talk through.
  • An AI tool that reads a customer message, sorts it, and drafts a reply for a person to approve. On a 40-message practice test it handled all 40 correctly, sent 6/6 emergencies to a person, and wrote 0 unsafe replies.
  • 8 ready-to-use office templates and an editable toolkit.
  • This proposal website, built and published with the help of AI tools.

The honest limit

The practice messages were ones I wrote myself. Before anyone relies on the AI tool, I’d test it on your real customer messages, with your team checking the answers. If it doesn’t meet the bar we agree on, it doesn’t go live.

The first 90 days

Days 1–30Learn first. Learn how customer messages are handled today, agree on my first jobs, and measure how things work now so we can compare later.
Days 31–60Take on regular tasks. Take over two regular tasks, then test the AI tool on real company messages before anyone relies on it.
Days 61–90Show the results. Choose what’s next. Try the AI tool on a small scale alongside the way things are done now, and compare the results with the starting measurement.

How we’ll judge the work

Four things, compared with the starting measurement from the first 30 days:

  • Reply time. How long a customer usually waits for a first reply from a person.
  • Complete details. How many messages arrive with everything needed to act.
  • Nothing forgotten. How many open requests have a person responsible and a date for the next step.
  • Time per message. Minutes spent on each message, including checking and fixing drafts.

AI tool costs: $400 a month, never more

The company provides and pays for two AI subscriptions in its own name: Claude Max ($200) and ChatGPT Pro with Codex ($200). No pay-as-you-go credits, add-ons, or usage fees. If a limit is reached, the work waits. Any change needs your written approval.
